What is this thing?

The short, no-nonsense version of what Taldea is — and what it is not.

What is Taldea?

Taldea is an app for short-lived photo groups . Someone shares a link — you join, drop what's happening right now, and the moment stays inside that group only.

How do groups work?

Groups start from a link or a QR. Everyone inside can post for a short window of time. When the group quiets down, it simply… goes quiet. No feeds, no history, no infinite scrolling.

What do I actually do here?

Join a group, share what's happening right now, and see what everyone else in that group is sharing too. That’s it. No profiles to set up, no followers to manage.

What Taldea is not

  • No likes and no follower counts.
  • No explore tab and no algorithmic feed.
  • No public profiles. No strangers scrolling your stuff.

If you’re not in the group, you don’t see anything from it.

Who sees my photos?

Only the people inside the group you joined. Groups are invite-only and always start from a link. Nothing is searchable or public.
