What is this thing?

The short version of what taldea does and does not do.

What is Taldea?

Taldea is an app for short-lived photo groups . Someone shares a link — on IG DM, WhatsApp, Telegram, or anywhere really. You join, drop what's happening right now, and then it's gone.

How do groups work?

Every group starts from a link or a QR. Everyone inside can post photos for a limited time. When the group is over, it goes quiet. No endless history to scroll.

What do I actually do here?

You create or join a group, share what is happening right now, and see what the others in that group are seeing too. That is it. No feeds to manage, no profile to curate.

What Taldea is not

  • No likes, no comments, no public follower counts.
  • No explore page or algorithmic feed.
  • No public profiles to browse.

If you are not in the group, you do not see what happens there.

Who sees my photos?

Only the people in the group you joined. Groups are invite-only and start from a link. We do not make your groups or photos searchable or public.
