Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Taldea account and what happens to your data when you do.

How to delete your account

You can request the deletion of your Taldea account at any time from inside the app.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your account and group memberships are removed immediately.
  • You are removed from all groups you were a member of.
  • Any group you created is deleted immediately.
  • Active content (photos) may remain available for up to 24 hours, due to the ephemeral nature of the product, and is then automatically deleted.

Data retention

  • Photos are automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Groups with no activity are automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • If you were the creator of a group, the group is deleted immediately when your account is deleted.

Taldea does not keep deleted accounts for active use. Any remaining technical data is only kept where required for security, legal or operational purposes, and is removed according to applicable laws.
