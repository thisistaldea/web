What is Taldea

Taldea is a mobile app to create and join short-lived groups of moments (photos). When you create a group, Taldea generates an invitation link and a QR code linked to that group. Anyone who has Taldea installed can join by tapping the link or scanning the QR code with any compatible camera or QR reader app, as long as the group is active.

In Taldea you share moments (photos) that can be seen inside the group for a limited time and then disappear. If a group goes 7 days in a row without any new moment, the group is deleted. By using the app, you accept these terms.

  • Moments can be viewed inside the group for a maximum of 24 hours.
  • After that, moments disappear.

How Taldea works (what it does NOT have)

Taldea is designed to be simple. This means the app leaves out many typical social features to focus on real-time sharing.

  • There are no likes or social reactions.
  • There is no commenting system.
  • There are no public follower counts or explore tabs.
  • You cannot upload content from your gallery. Moments are captured with Taldea's camera, in the moment.
  • You cannot edit a moment once it has been posted.
  • Taldea is not a memory archive. It is about what is happening now, and then it goes away.

Groups, people and invitations

Groups in Taldea are created from invitation links and QR codes. The person who creates the group is its creator or admin. To join a group you need to use an invitation.

To understand which invitations work and to assign rewards or extra views, Taldea may generate individual invitation links per person and per group. From the perspective of other people in the group, invitations stay anonymous at interface level.

The link or QR code you have can be shared in any channel (messaging apps, social networks, etc.). If you share your link publicly, people you do not know may join. The use and distribution of that link is your responsibility and the responsibility of the other members of the group.

The creator can regenerate the main invitation link of a group so that a previous one stops working. When this happens, Taldea can generate new individual links associated with that group, which will be used from that moment on.

  • Only the creator can remove other people from the group.
  • Anyone can leave the group at any time.
  • To join a group you either scan an invitation QR with any compatible camera or QR reader app, or open an invitation link someone has shared with you.
  • Each member can have their own link to invite other people to the same group.
  • We use this information internally to understand which invitations have worked and to assign rewards, such as extra views.
  • We do not publicly show who invited whom.
  • Other people will not see your name tied to something like 'invited X'.
