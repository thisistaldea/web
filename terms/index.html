What is Taldea

Taldea is a private group-sharing app built around temporary Moments and real-life connections.

Unlike traditional social networks, Taldea is designed for private groups rather than public audiences.

Moments are shared with the people who matter to you, stay around for a limited time and then make room for new ones.

By using Taldea, you acknowledge that:

  • Moments can be viewed inside the group for a limited period of time.
  • Active Moments may receive Echos from group members.
  • Taldea is built for the present, not for permanent publishing.

You can use Taldea if:

  • You are at least 13 years old.
  • If the law in your country requires a higher minimum age for online services, you meet that age.
  • You are legally able to enter into these Terms.

How Taldea works (what it does NOT have)

Taldea is designed to be different from many traditional social platforms.

Today Taldea has:

  • No public profiles.
  • No follower counts.
  • No recommendation algorithms.
  • No public feeds.
  • No popularity metrics such as likes.

Instead, Taldea focuses on private groups, temporary Moments and lightweight conversations through Echos.

Groups, people and invitations

Groups in Taldea are created from invitation links and QR codes. The person who creates the group is its creator or admin. To join a group you need to use an invitation.

To understand which invitations work, Taldea may generate individual invitation links per person and per group. From the perspective of other people in the group, invitations stay anonymous at interface level.

The link or QR code you have can be shared in any channel (messaging apps, social networks, etc.). If you share your link publicly, people you do not know may join. The use and distribution of that link is your responsibility and the responsibility of the other members of the group.

The creator can regenerate the main invitation link of a group so that a previous one stops working. When this happens, Taldea can generate new individual links associated with that group, which will be used from that moment on.

  • Only the creator can remove other people from the group.
  • Anyone can leave the group at any time.
  • To join a group you either scan an invitation QR with any compatible camera or QR reader app, or open an invitation link someone has shared with you.
  • Each member can have their own link to invite other people to the same group.
  • We may use invitation and participation information to understand how groups grow and to improve Taldea.
  • We do not publicly show who invited whom.
  • Other people will not see your name tied to something like 'invited X'.

Echos

They allow group members to react, answer or continue conversations around active Moments.

Like Moments, Echos are designed around the temporary nature of Taldea and are not intended to become permanent records.
