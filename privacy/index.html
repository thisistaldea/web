Privacy Policy

Introduction

We created Taldea so people can share Moments with their groups, not so we can build a large database of personal memories.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the rights you have regarding your data.

By using Taldea, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Quick Summary (TL;DR)

  • We only collect the information necessary to operate, secure and improve Taldea.
  • Your groups are accessed through private invitation links and are not publicly searchable.
  • Moments and Echos are designed to be temporary.
  • We do not use your Moments or Echos for advertising purposes.
  • Moments and Echos are protected so they remain accessible only to authorized participants within a group.
  • We use limited product analytics to understand how Taldea is used and improve the experience.
  • You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data where required by law.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your account data and active content as far as technically possible and legally permitted.

What data we collect

When you use Taldea, we may process the following categories of information.

Account data

Information received from your sign-in provider, such as Apple or Google.

This may include:

  • User identifier
  • Display name
  • Email address (if provided by the authentication provider)

Group and activity data

Information related to your use of Taldea, including:

  • Groups you create or join
  • Invitations you send or receive
  • Group membership information
  • Feature usage and interaction events

Technical and device data

Information required for the service to function properly, including:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Content data

Content you create and share inside Taldea, including:

  • Moments
  • Echos
  • Group-related content

Support and communication data

Information you provide when contacting us for support or feedback.

Protected Content

Taldea is designed to limit access to the content shared inside groups.

Moments and Echos are protected using privacy technologies intended to ensure that content remains accessible only to authorized participants within a group.

We do not use Moments or Echos to build advertising profiles, recommendation systems or public activity feeds.

The purpose of these protections is simple:

Your content is for your group, not for us.

How and why we use your data

We process personal data under applicable privacy laws, including the GDPR and Spanish data protection legislation.

We use your information for the following purposes.

To provide the service

This includes:

  • Creating and managing accounts
  • Creating and joining groups
  • Sharing Moments
  • Publishing Echos
  • Delivering content to group members
  • Operating the core functionality of Taldea

To keep Taldea secure

We use information to:

  • Prevent abuse and fraud
  • Protect users and groups
  • Investigate reports and violations of our Terms

To improve Taldea

We may use limited analytics and product data to:

  • Understand how features are used
  • Improve usability and reliability
  • Identify bugs and technical issues

To comply with legal obligations

We may process information when required by law or when necessary to protect users, Taldea or third parties.

How long we keep your data

We only keep information for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

Moments

Moments are designed to be temporary and are generally removed after their intended availability period ends.

Echos

Echos are linked to Moments and may be removed when the related Moment is no longer available.

Inactive groups

Groups that remain inactive according to Taldea's product rules may be removed together with associated content.

Account deletion

When you delete your account, we remove your account information and active content as far as technically feasible and legally permitted.

Certain information may be retained where required by law, security obligations or dispute resolution purposes.

Aggregated information

Anonymous or aggregated usage information may be retained for analytical purposes.

This information cannot reasonably be used to identify individual users.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only with the following categories of recipients.

Service providers

Companies that help us operate Taldea, such as:

  • Hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Security providers

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services.

Other users

Members of your groups can see the Moments and Echos you choose to share within those groups.

We do not publicly expose group content.

Authorities

We may disclose information when required by law, court order or other valid legal process.

International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area.

Where this occurs, we use appropriate safeguards, including contractual protections approved under applicable privacy laws.

Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights including:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict certain processing activities
  • Object to specific uses of your information
  • Request data portability where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@taldea.app

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or another competent supervisory authority.

Minors

Taldea is intended for people aged 13 and older.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from someone who does not meet the applicable age requirements, we may take steps to remove that information and restrict access to the service.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to discuss responsible online behaviour with younger users.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are significant, we may notify users through the app and, where appropriate, request acceptance of the updated version before continuing to use Taldea.

Previous versions of this Privacy Policy may be retained for legal and compliance purposes.

Privacy Philosophy

Taldea was built around a simple idea:

The people you share with matter more than the audience you reach.

We believe private moments should stay private.

We believe temporary moments should not live forever.

And we believe your content should belong to your group, not become a product.

That philosophy guides how we design Taldea and how we handle your data.
